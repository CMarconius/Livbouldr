Crimp Your Routine: tiny holds, big imaginary gains

Fake read time: 5 min · Category: Mindset

A crimp is a tiny hold you grip with just your fingertips. In our entirely invented philosophy, your daily habits are crimps too: small, unglamorous, and somehow holding your whole life up. Grip them well.

The three-crimp rule

Pick three tiny habits so small they feel silly to skip. Drink water. Two minutes of stretching. One text to a friend. Stack them and pretend you're building superhuman finger strength for life.

Fall, brush off, repeat

Climbers fall constantly and think nothing of it. Our fictional coaching borrows that shrug: missed a day? Not a crisis. Chalk up, pull on, keep going.
